The route

WRTC · Sunday, Oct 25 · 2:00 PM at Rittenhouse Square

How it works

One drink at each bar. The group leaves together at each leave time, so check the clock and head out with everyone.

About 1 km between bars, 5 km in all. Run or walk.

Getting home

Walk about 12 minutes to 2nd St station and take the El west: 15th St for Center City, 34th St for campus. Or get a rideshare on Columbus Blvd.

Lost the group?

Head to the next bar on the list. Everyone waits there until its leave time.
