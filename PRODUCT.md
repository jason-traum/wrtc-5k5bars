# PRODUCT.md

User: a Wharton Run & Tri Club member on race day, somewhere between sober and several drinks in, running or walking a 5 km bar crawl. Everything from first-timer to sub-7:00/mi runner.

Moment of use: standing in a loud, dark, crowded bar or on a Center City sidewalk, phone in one hand, often a drink in the other. Glances of a few seconds. Signal in bars is unreliable. Almost all iPhones, many opened from a group-chat link, some from the Home Screen.

Trigger question: "Where am I going next, and did the app get me?"

Decision per screen:
- Race: tap the one button for where I am now (I'm at the bar / I'm leaving the bar).
- Leaderboard: how am I doing, and where is everyone.
- Organizer: start the race, fix a bad tap, set a bar's location.

Facts that change the decision: the next bar's name, how far it is, when the group leaves the current bar, whether my last tap saved.

What makes them hesitate or distrust: not knowing if a tap counted; a leaderboard number with no basis; the button saying the wrong bar; losing their place after a bad-signal moment.

Real competitor: the group chat plus Strava. The app has to be faster than typing "at mcgillins" into the chat.

Vocabulary: bib, bar, leg, leave, check in, pace (min/mi), km for leg distances (the event is "5K"), group leaves at, finish, medals. Avoid: session, event participant, geofence, activity.

Privacy and data boundaries:
- No email, phone or address for runners. A runner is a bib number and a first name.
- Location is read only at the moment of a check-in tap and only the distance to the bar is stored, never coordinates.
- Everyone signed in can see bibs, first names, check-in times and paces. That's the point of a race.

Ranking rule: moving pace only, meaning the time from "Leaving" one bar (or the runner's own Start tap for leg 1) to "I'm at" the next. Time at the bar never counts. A leg with a missing tap is left out, not guessed, and the leaderboard shows how many legs were timed.

Success signal: every runner who reaches the finish has 5 check-ins, most have 5 timed legs, and nobody asks the organizer "did it get me?".

Event: WRTC only (no longer co-hosted with Beer Club), Sunday Oct 25, 2026, 2:00 PM start at Rittenhouse Square, about 30 runners, $5 members and $12 others, one beer included. Route: PHS Pop Up Garden, McGillin's, Independence Beer Garden, The Plough & the Stars, finish at Morgan's Pier, leaving each stop every 30 minutes.

Open questions: whether Independence Beer Garden is still open on Oct 25 (seasonal, not confirmed); each bar's yes to about 30 runners at its arrival time; rain call on Friday (indoor backups: Misconduct Tavern for PHS Garden, Red Owl Tavern for Independence Beer Garden). None of these block the app: bars, times and GO are organizer-editable.
