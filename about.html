A proposal for a joint Beer Club and WRTC event: a 5K bar crawl from Rittenhouse Square to Morgan's Pier, with five bars about 1 km apart, one drink at each, and about 3 hours start to finish. Run or walk any leg. Everyone leaves each bar at the same time, so the group stays together.

The date is set for Sunday, Oct 25. The bars, the price and how drinks work are still open.

The app

The race-day app is already built. This is how runners would get it:

  1. Scan the code or tap the link. It opens in your browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the menu and tap Add to Home screen.
  3. On race day, join with your bib number and first name, then check in when you get to each bar.

Drinks

Not decided yet. The two options:

Pay as you go

Everyone orders and pays at each bar like normal, for whatever they want. The club has nothing to collect or settle, but the lines move slower.

Tickets

An upfront fee buys five tickets, one color per bar, and each bar bills the club. It's the fastest at the bar, but the club handles the money and covers no-shows.

Draft rules

  • It's a social run, not a race. The streets stay open and there are no course marshals.
  • You take part at your own risk.
  • Stay on sidewalks and cross at crosswalks.
  • 21+ only. Bring ID; bars will card.
  • Every drink is optional. Each bar has water and a non-alcoholic option.
  • Walk any leg you want. If you drop out, tell the sweep.
